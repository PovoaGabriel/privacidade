Privacy Policy — Esteira

Last updated: 2026-10-04

Esteira is a private tool operated by [OPERATOR NAME] to schedule and manage the operator's own YouTube channels. It has no public users.

What we access

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool reads and writes data of the owner's own YouTube channels: videos, thumbnails, playlists, video metadata and YouTube Analytics for those channels. It does not access viewer data, comments or any other person's account.

How we use it

To upload and schedule the owner's videos, to keep a record of their status and to produce reports for the owner. Data is not used for advertising and is not used to train machine-learning models.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on systems controlled by the operator. OAuth tokens and cached channel data are kept while the channel is connected and are deleted within 7 days after access is revoked.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose this data to third parties.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
